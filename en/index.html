BEFORE BRINGING YOUR CLOTHES

What you might
want to ask.

How much does the service cost?

The price is confirmed based on the garment, quantity and requested service. Ask before bringing your clothes to find out the conditions.

When will my garments be ready?

Turnaround time is confirmed when we review your request. Message us if you need a specific date.

What alterations can I ask about?

Describe the adjustment or repair you need. Availability depends on the fabric and condition of the garment, so we inspect it before confirming.

Can any garment be washed or dried?

We check the care instructions and fabric type. Ask about delicate garments or special care instructions before using the service.
